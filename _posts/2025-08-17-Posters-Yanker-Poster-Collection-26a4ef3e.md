Collection consists of political, propaganda, and social issue posters, handbills, and a few bumper stickers. About 1,500 of the posters were produced in the United States, with the remaining issued in more than 50 other countries and the United Nations. The major themes portrayed were often considered radical protest pieces in the 1960s and 1970s. Topics include ecology and environment, the Vietnam War, women's equality, civil rights of African Americans, nuclear power, political participation and elections at the national and local level, marches and demonstrations, events and anniversaries, and personalities such as Che Guevara and Richard Nixon. Some are one-of-a-kind images intended for posting on the spur of the moment. Others, by well-organized pressure groups and major political parties, are slick and polished.
