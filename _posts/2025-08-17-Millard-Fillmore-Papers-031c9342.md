Chiefly correspondence of Fillmore relating to slavery; the Compromise of 1850; the Fugitive Slave Law (1850); John Brown's Raid, 1859, Harpers Ferry, West Virginia; the Whig Party; and congressional politics.  Individuals mentioned include Thomas Hart Benton, John C. Calhoun, and William Henry Harrison.  Correspondents include Philip Richard Fendall, Solomon G. Haven, and Humphrey Marshall.  Includes several letters of and an obituary notice for Fillmore's daughter, Mary Abigail Fillmore, as well as a detailed index compiled in 1925 to volumes 1-44 of the Millard Fillmore papers in the Buffalo History Museum, Buffalo, N.Y.
