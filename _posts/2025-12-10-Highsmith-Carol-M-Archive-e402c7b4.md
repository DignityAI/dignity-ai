The Carol M. Highsmith Archive features photographs documenting primarily the United States through more than 70,000 photos. The collection, which spans from 1980 to the present, includes landmark buildings, architectural renovation projects, landscapes and cityscapes, people and cultural events in a combined portrait of daily life. The archive is expected to grow to more than 100,000 photographs.
