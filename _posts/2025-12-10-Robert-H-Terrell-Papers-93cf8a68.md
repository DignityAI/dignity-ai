The papers of educator and jurist Robert Herberton Terrell (1857-1925) consist of approximately 2,750 items, comprising 2,977 images, all of which were digitized from four reels of previously produced microfilm. Spanning the years 1870-1954, with the bulk of the material concentrated in the period 1884-1925, the collection contains correspondence, speeches and writings, clippings, printed matter, and miscellaneous items.
