Summary: The collection, assembed by Theodore Dwight (1796-1866) includes the autobiographical manuscript written in 1831 by Omar ibn Said (1770-1863 or 1864), in Arabic. This is the only known American slave memoir, written in Arabic, while still in slavery. Includes texts and letters, some written in Arabic, some in English (and translations into English of some texts).
